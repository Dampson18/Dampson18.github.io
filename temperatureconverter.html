Understanding Temperature Scales

Celsius (°C)

The Celsius scale is based on the freezing point (0°C) and boiling point (100°C) of water. It's widely used around the world.

Fahrenheit (°F)

The Fahrenheit scale defines the freezing point of water at 32°F and the boiling point at 212°F. Primarily used in the United States.

Kelvin (K)

The Kelvin scale is an absolute thermodynamic temperature scale. 0 K (absolute zero) is the point where molecular motion ceases. It's used in scientific contexts.
